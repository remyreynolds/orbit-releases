What Orbit does

Three things, done properly.

◐

A wallpaper that moves

Real 8K photographs, animated from their own pixels. Nebula gas drifts while the stars stay sharp. Lakes ripple, clouds pass, and the camera glides slowly across wide panoramas. It changes every 30 minutes and dims itself at night.

✦

Your day, on the right

A glass panel sits on your desktop, under your apps and above your icons. Next move, due-today count, school, inbox, calendar and music, all in one look. Drag widgets into any order and drag the edge to make it wider.

⧉

Connect your stuff

Sign in to Google, Microsoft 365, Canvas, Todoist and Spotify, add any calendar link, RSS feed or MCP server. Everything lands in one place and ranks itself by priority and due date.

🔒

Private by design.

Orbit has no servers and no accounts of its own. It runs on your computer and talks to your services directly. Sign-ins are encrypted in your system keychain. Mail is read as sender and subject only, never the message body. Nothing you connect is sent to us, because there’s no “us” in the middle.

Questions

Does it slow my computer down?

No. The wallpaper renders at 30 frames per second in the GPU’s low-power mode and stops completely while a full-screen app covers it. You can also switch any scene to a still image.

Will my desktop icons and windows still work?

Yes. The wallpaper sits behind your icons, and the dashboard sits on the desktop under every app window, so it never gets in your way. Hide it any time from the menu bar (or the system tray on Windows).

Does it work with an ultrawide or several monitors?

Yes. Each display gets its own moving wallpaper, framed to fit its shape, and the dashboard lives on your main display.

Do I need Claude, Codex or any other app running?

No. Orbit runs on its own. If you use apps like these, Orbit can show their usage limits, but it never needs them.

Can I use my own wallpaper?

Yes. Upload any photo in Controls. It stays on your computer, and you can choose fill or fit.
